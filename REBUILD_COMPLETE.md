## 🎯 **Design System**

### Colors
- **Primary:** Blue (#3B82F6)
- **Background:** Dark Gray (#111827)
- **Cards:** Gray-800 (#1F2937)
- **Text:** White & Gray-400
- **Accents:** Purple gradient

### Typography
- **Headings:** Bold, 2xl to 5xl
- **Body:** Regular, base to xl
- **Font:** System fonts (clean & readable)

### Spacing
- **Sections:** py-20 (consistent)
- **Cards:** p-6 to p-8
- **Grid gaps:** gap-8
- **Max width:** max-w-7xl

### Components
- **Rounded corners:** rounded-xl
- **Borders:** border-gray-700
- **Shadows:** shadow-lg on cards
- **Transitions:** 200-300ms duration
